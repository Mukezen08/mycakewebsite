What We Believe

The Principles That Drive Every Bake

Six beliefs that shape every decision we make — from ingredient selection to how we package your order.

How It Started

Born from a Chocolate Obsession

YNotABite didn't start with a business plan — it started with a question. "Why settle for an ordinary brownie when extraordinary is just a better ingredient away?"

Nirupamaa spent months perfecting ratios, sourcing real couverture chocolate, testing baking times down to the minute. What emerged wasn't just a recipe — it was a philosophy of refusal: refusing shortcuts, refusing mediocrity, refusing to ship anything less than perfect.

That same refusal drives every brownie we bake today.

Our Commitment

Promises We Never Break

🚫
No Pre-Baking

We never bake ahead and store. Your order triggers the oven. Every brownie arrives at peak freshness — always.

🚫
No Compound Chocolate

Real couverture chocolate only. We never use compound chocolate — even though it's cheaper. The taste difference is undeniable.

🚫
No Artificial Flavours

Every flavour you taste — matcha, caramel, rose — comes from the real ingredient. Always natural, always genuine.

🚫
No Compromised Packaging

The box matters as much as the brownie. We invest in packaging that protects freshness and creates an unboxing moment.

The Headliners

Star Ingredients

The ingredients that define the YNotABite difference — what you taste most, what we obsess over most.

🍫
The Foundation
Couverture Dark Chocolate

We use real couverture chocolate with a minimum 65% cocoa content and high cocoa butter. This is what gives our brownies their signature glossy top and deep, complex chocolate flavour — impossible to replicate with compound chocolate.

65%+ Cocoa
🧈
The Texture
High-Fat Premium Butter

Brownies live or die by their texture. We use high-fat butter (82%+ fat content) — not regular table butter — because the extra fat creates that silky, melt-in-the-mouth interior that defines a truly fudgy brownie. The difference is immediate and unmistakable.

82%+ Fat Content
🌿
The Colour & Depth
Dutch-Process Cocoa Powder

Dutch-process cocoa gives our brownies their deep mahogany colour and a smoother, richer chocolate flavour compared to natural cocoa. It's the professional baker's choice — and ours. The result: a darker, more intense brownie with beautiful colour all the way through.

Alkalized Process
🥚
The Structure
Free-Range Farm Eggs

Eggs do three jobs in a brownie: they bind, they add richness, and they create that essential crinkle top. We use free-range eggs with richer, more golden yolks. The result is a more flavourful batter and a brownie with better structure and that beautiful shiny crust everyone loves.

Free-Range Only
🌸
The Signature
Ceremonial Grade Matcha

For our matcha brownies we use nothing less than Japanese ceremonial-grade matcha. The difference in colour (vivid green vs dull army-green) and flavour (complex, sweet-grassy vs bitter) is stark. Our matcha drizzle is instantly recognisable — vivid, natural, unmistakably premium.

Japanese Ceremonial Grade
🍮
The Swirl
Handmade Salted Caramel

We make caramel from scratch in-house — sugar, cream, a hint of sea salt. No store-bought sauce. The result is a caramel that actually smells like caramel and doesn't turn cloying or artificial. You can taste the difference in every swirl.

Made In-House

Dietary Information

Allergens & Special Diets

Eggless Available
Most brownie varieties can be made eggless on request. Mention this in your order notes for a perfectly adapted recipe.
Contains: Gluten, Dairy, Eggs, Nuts
Standard brownies contain wheat flour, butter, eggs, and may contain tree nuts. Always inform us of severe allergies before ordering.
Nut-Free Options
Many varieties available without nuts. Please specify in your order and we'll ensure a nut-free preparation environment.
What We Never Use
Artificial food colouring
Artificial flavour extracts
Compound chocolate or cocoa-flavoured fat
Preservatives or shelf-life extenders
Margarine or vegetable shortening
Stale or pre-stored nuts

Have a specific dietary concern? WhatsApp us before ordering and we'll advise on the safest option for you.
